Loopstar

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Loopstar does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • It makes no network requests while you play and works fully offline.

What is saved on your device

Your progress is saved only on your own device: best score, stardust, unlocked comets, missions, daily reward streak and your sound, music and vibration settings. It never leaves the device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can also erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the game's settings.

Permissions

The Android app asks for vibration, used for tap feedback, and for standard internet access, which the app framework requires. The game does not use the internet permission to send or receive your data.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
