# Play Store listing draft

**App name** (30 max): Loopstar

**Short description** (80 max): Sling a comet from planet to planet. One tap. Don't burn out.

**Full description**

Your comet circles a planet. Tap once to let go.

Fly into the next planet's ring and it catches you. Miss, and you burn out. That is the whole game, and it is very hard to put down.

- One-tap play. Learn it in five seconds, play it with one thumb.
- Swift streaks. Let go before a full lap to build a multiplier of up to x5.
- Bounce shots. Bank off the walls for bonus points and hard-to-reach stardust.
- Keep moving. A red tide rises from below and it gets faster.
- New hazards as you climb: drifting planets, mines, tighter rings.
- Shields and magnets to pick up along the way.
- Eight comets to unlock with the stardust you collect.
- Missions and a daily reward that grows each day you come back.
- Works offline. No account. No data collected.
