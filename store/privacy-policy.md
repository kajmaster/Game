# Loopstar privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Loopstar does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
- Your progress (best score, stardust, unlocked comets, settings) is saved only on your own device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- The game works fully offline and makes no network requests while you play.

If a future version adds ads, purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
